What the three answers mean

Green

The deal fits a program. Send your client the application and the Forge team submits it with a document checklist in hand.

Yellow

Close, with questions. The Forge team reaches out to you or the client for a few specifics before anything is submitted.

Red

Not a fit today. You know in a minute, not in three weeks, and you can tell your client the truth.
